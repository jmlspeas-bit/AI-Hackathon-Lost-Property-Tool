# Lost property office facts

These facts describe how the lost property office runs today. The data files are a copy of the office's spreadsheets taken on the morning of Tuesday, September 29, 2026, so treat that date as today when you test your agent.

## What do the data files include?

Staff logged Monday's items between 8:00 and 9:30 a.m. on Tuesday, September 29, so everything handed in up to the end of Monday, September 28, is in `found_items.csv`. Items found on Tuesday won't be logged until Wednesday morning.

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| `found_items.csv` | • Every item logged from July 1 to September 29, 2026<br>• The `status` column shows whether each item is held, claimed, returned, disposed of, or transferred |
| `lost_reports.csv` | • Forms submitted from August 30 to September 29, 2026, that haven't been matched to an item |
| `claims.csv` | • Every claim opened from July 1 to September 29, 2026, including rejected claims |

## Where does staff time go?

The three staff members work about 480 hours a month between them. Most of that time goes on four tasks.

| Task | Time per item | Hours a month |
|---|---|---|
| Searching for a match for each form and replying | • 12 minutes per form | • 220 |
| Checking proof of ownership and closing claims | • 20 minutes per claim | • 77 |
| Logging items each morning | • 4 minutes per item | • 50 |
| Weekly clearance of items past their holding period | • Not tracked per item | • 24 |

## What does the office cost?

Staff cost the authority $38 an hour, including benefits. At 480 hours a month, that's $18,240 a month for the office's three staff.

Riders pay shipping fees themselves, so shipping doesn't appear in the office's costs. The fees are $9.50 for standard shipping and $18.00 for tracked and insured shipping.

## How does the office handle data today?

Found items, lost reports, and claims are kept in three spreadsheets in the authority's Google Workspace account. Only the three office staff can open them.

Riders submit lost reports through a form on the authority's website. Staff contact riders by email, or by phone if the rider asks. The authority's IT team requires any outside service that stores rider data to keep it in the United States.

## How does the board approve new services?

The authority's board meets on the first Thursday of each month. A new service needs board approval before it goes live, and the board asks for its monthly running cost and how it handles mistakes before voting.
